Mingfeng ZHA

PhD Student · The Hong Kong Polytechnic University

Hi, there! My Chinese name is 查明峰. I am a first-year PhD student at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. Previously, I earned my master's degree at the Center For Future Media, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, led by Prof. Heng Tao Shen, under the supervision of Prof. Guoqing Wang. During undergraduate studies, I collaborated with Prof. Wenbin Qian. My research interests primarily include computer vision and multimodal learning (e.g., unified framework).

Please feel free to contact me by email if you're interested in discussing potential collaborations or partnering on research projects.
